---

# 1. PROJECT ROLE

You are a senior full-stack architect, UI/UX designer, AI systems engineer, database architect, security engineer, QA engineer, and DevOps engineer.

Your task is to design and build a **complete, production-quality AI-powered e-commerce platform** from the ground up.

Do not create a static demo.

Do not create mock buttons that do nothing.

Do not create disconnected pages.

Every important UI element must connect to a real application flow, API, database operation, or clearly defined service.

The final application must behave like a professional modern e-commerce platform.

# 2. CORE PRODUCT VISION

Build a modern e-commerce website with a familiar shopping experience similar in usability and information architecture to established platforms such as Bagisto-based storefronts.

The website must look and behave like a normal e-commerce website.

The major differentiator is a single integrated:

# "AI SHOPPING PARTNER"

The user must not need to understand individual AI agents.

Internally, the system may use multiple specialized AI capabilities, but externally the user interacts with **one unified AI assistant**.

The AI Shopping Partner should help users:

* Discover products
* Search naturally
* Understand products
* Compare products
* Create shopping goals
* Analyze spending patterns
* Understand affordability
* Plan purchases
* Predict future needs
* Optimize carts
* Find alternatives
* Track prices
* Manage wishlists
* Organize group shopping
* Plan gifts
* Track recurring purchases
* Understand post-purchase needs
* Make better purchasing decisions

The AI must not simply encourage users to spend more.

It should sometimes recommend:

> "You may want to wait."

> "This purchase is outside your current planned budget."

> "You already own a similar product."

> "A lower-cost alternative may satisfy your requirement."

The AI should behave like a **trusted shopping partner**, not an advertising engine.
